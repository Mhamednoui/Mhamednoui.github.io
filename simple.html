Services

2. IoT / firmware security review and CRA readiness

A hands-on review of your device: UART and debug access, flash extraction and firmware unpacking, then stored secrets, the update path and exposed services. Useful before the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) obligations apply.
Typical deliverable: a written report with findings ranked by risk, recommended fixes and a gap list against the CRA requirements, to support your own conformity work. Proof: ISP router reverse engineering (public write-up), 4G router root, intrusion detection thesis.

3. PCB design and hardware bring-up support

2-layer boards in KiCad, checked with design-rule and electrical checks and SPICE simulation for analogue stages, plus firmware and debugging support when the first boards arrive.
Typical deliverable: the KiCad project, Gerber, BOM and pick-and-place files, and a short check report. Proof: PCB design and AI-assisted pipeline; arcade controller board (in development).

4. Generative-AI production and local AI setup

Print-ready images produced with locally hosted models, or a private AI setup on your own hardware: image and video workflows and local LLMs, with no data sent to outside services.
Typical deliverable: finished, print-ready assets, or a documented workflow installed and tested on your machine. Proof: generative-AI production (paid client work).

ISP Router Reverse Engineering (Realtek RTL8676S)

2026 · public write-up

  • Got a shell over UART, then dumped the flash two ways: over TFTP, and by desoldering the SPI-NOR chip (chip-off). Both dumps were byte-identical.

  • UART · CH341A · SPI-NOR · binwalk · LZMA · write-up on GitHub

Multi-Domain Intrusion Detection System for Autonomous Vehicles

Master's thesis · 2025–2026

  • Four ML detectors (network, CAN bus, GPS, LiDAR), with a fusion engine that combines them into 5 threat levels and responds in tiers.
  • Live 5,400-cycle simulation on unfiltered traffic: 96.6% recall, 93.8% accuracy, 90.1% precision (caught 2,319 of 2,400 attack cycles).
  • XGBoost detectors trained on public datasets (CICIDS2017, CICIoV2024, AV-GPS-Dataset, KITTI with 3 synthetic LiDAR attacks), a temporal correlator, SHAP explanations, and CAN windowing that cuts model calls 50×. CAN detector: 0 errors on 281,644 held-out CICIoV2024 frames.
  • Thesis graded Very Good (Tunisia Polytechnic School); a paper from it is under review.
  • Python · XGBoost · scikit-learn · SHAP · pandas

4G Router Root (ZTE ZX297520V3 platform)

2026 · own device
